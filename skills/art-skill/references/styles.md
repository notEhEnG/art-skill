The original six recipes have four photo examples each. Every additional recipe has one reviewed example. A palette is prompt guidance rather than a hard tool control. Keep a reviewed anchor and the same recipe for every derivative in a pack, then inspect the results.

Some styles change framing or deliberately abstract the source. Follow the selected recipe and record that transformation. Thermal and X-ray treatments are artistic, schematic images are illustrative, and detail studies do not establish scientific facts.

See [quality.md](quality.md) for review guidance and each recipe's `review_checks` and `limitations` for medium-specific details.
