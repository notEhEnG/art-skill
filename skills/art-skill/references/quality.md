# Visual acceptance

Check every output against the brief and accepted anchor. Record concrete defects rather than a numerical “style match” claim without a calibrated measurement.

| Recipe | Look for | Do not promise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASCII/pixels | Glyph-like stippling or intentional blocks; restrained palette; readable silhouette | Valid ASCII text, exact glyphs, pixel-grid correctness, game-ready sprite tiles |
| Sketch/scribble | Gestural line hierarchy, visible paper, controlled accent marks | An exact artist's drawing or preserved strokes after edits |
| Digital impressionism | Broken-color marks, warm light/cool shadows, consistent brush scale | Pixel-identical brushwork or exact geometry |
| Lo-fi film | Restrained grain, soft focus, gentle vignette and subtle light leaks | Real camera/film simulation or exact colorimetry |
| Generative flow | Coherent contours, layered filaments and intentional empty space | Executable algorithms, seed reproducibility, seamless tiling |
| Off-white | Warm neutral values, subtle texture, sparse composition | Accessible UI text contrast or a usable website as a bitmap |

| Watercolor Wash | paper is visibly part of the image; cloud layers remain distinct; translucent washes instead of opaque impasto | Paper and pigment behavior are visual approximations, not a physical watercolor simulation. |
| Gouache Poster | opaque matte coverage; distinct foreground and background layers; tree rhythm and path survive simplification | A poster aesthetic does not supply print layouts or guarantee exact typography. |
| Linocut | two-tone tonal hierarchy; directional carved-looking marks; negative space separates the path from foliage | Raster art only; no cutting template, separated plates or carve-ready geometry is supplied. |
| Risograph | limited color layers; restrained consistent edge offset; roofline and windows remain readable | No actual spot-color separations, calibrated inks or press-ready print files are generated. |
| Stained Glass | cloud silhouette organizes the pane layout; leading is coherent; translucent-looking surfaces with restrained highlights | No fabrication pattern, cut lines or structurally validated glazing design is supplied. |
| Cyanotype | blue and pale-paper tonal family; garden depth is readable; foliage is structured rather than random silhouettes | This is an image aesthetic, not a real photographic exposure or calibrated cyanotype process. |
| Clay Diorama | clay-like surfaces and rounded forms; windows and railing retain their rhythm; lighting supports a coherent miniature scene | Raster visualization only; no 3D model, mesh, construction plan or physically accurate material is supplied. |
| Felt Collage | fibrous textile surfaces; layered cut shapes; garden slope and conifers remain recognizable | No fabric cutting template or stitch pattern is supplied. |
| Embroidery | fabric ground remains visible; stitch directions describe cloud volume; light and shadow cloud masses stay distinct | No machine embroidery file, thread specification or repeatable stitch geometry is generated. |
| Ink Wash | bright paper preserves cloud highlights; large tonal masses remain coherent; restrained washes without dense decorative outlines | Wash edges and tonal detail may drift during edits; physical ink diffusion is not simulated. |
| Geometric Facets | low horizon stays clear; large planes precede small detail; flat color hierarchy without photographic gradients | Raster illustration only; no vector paths or exact polygon topology is supplied. |
| Ceramic Mosaic | cloud and reflection bands remain readable; grout separates coherent tile groups; surface has tile-like material cues | No tile schedule, fabrication pattern, seamless texture or exact tessellation is supplied. |

For transparent cutouts, verify edges and that transparent pixels exist. For requested text, inspect every word. If exact lettering is required, flag errors and propose a separate deterministic typography step.

An inspection record states asset ID, checks performed, defect, severity, and whether repair is needed. A missing viewing tool means the visual check is unverified, not passed.

## Optical, material, dream and glow recipes

| Recipe | Look for | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aquatic Caustics | Caustic light follows a coherent watery surface; Distortion is localized and the horizon stays readable; Clear aqua highlights retain detail | Refraction and caustics are visual approximations; no physically validated optical simulation or seamless water texture is supplied. |
| Chiaroscuro | Strong light-dark hierarchy organizes the image; The focal shape survives shadow compression; Illuminated edges remain controlled rather than broadly overexposed | Fine shadow details may be deliberately lost; this is an artistic tonal treatment rather than recovered photographic information. |
| Chrome Futurism | Chrome reflections describe the original forms; The source silhouette and structural rhythm remain visible; Specular highlights avoid broad clipped patches | A contemporary chrome interpretation, not an art-historical reconstruction or a usable 3D material. |
| Ethereal Core | Natural source landmarks remain recognizable; Mist and halation create depth without covering everything; Light flecks remain sparse and subordinate to the landscape | Atmospheric fantasy treatment; it cannot restore hidden detail and does not certify a photographic location or physical lighting conditions. |
| Gel-Lit | Cool and warm light directions remain distinct; The subject stays readable through the colored lighting; Highlights and shadows describe surface planes | A lighting aesthetic; it does not specify a calibrated light rig, product color accuracy or a physical miniature. |
| Hyper Bloom | The original garden layout remains visible; Blooms feel integrated with existing plants; Translucent petals and glow stay distinct from general blur | Flower forms and colors are imagined; this is not a botanical record or a guarantee of realistic plant morphology. |
| Hyperglow | Spectral halos follow the source shapes; Bright contours are separated from dark negative space; Glow retains tonal structure instead of washing out the whole image | A raster light effect; no HDR luminance, emissive material, animation or calibrated spectral behavior is provided. |
| Macro Microscopy | A botanical detail from the source is identifiable; Fine membranes and translucent layers establish the macro treatment; The result is documented as a detail study rather than a preserved wide scene | Cell-like structures are imagined; the image cannot identify species, demonstrate biological anatomy or substitute for microscopy. |
| Modern Gradient | Large blended color fields establish the image; Source structure survives intentional abstraction; Fine grain is restrained and gradients remain smooth | The scene is intentionally abstracted; no exact color profile, banding-free display guarantee or vector gradient mesh is supplied. |
| Retro Glow | Airbrushed forms and star-filter accents establish the retro mood; The original composition remains recognizable; Glints remain sparse and do not obscure the subject | A nostalgic illustration aesthetic, not a calibrated film process or an exact reconstruction of any historical advertisement. |
| Technical Schematic | Roofline, window rhythm and perspective remain coherent; Thin construction lines and hatching are clear; No fabricated dimensions or illegible annotation clutter | An illustrative raster plate, not CAD, a dimensioned drawing, a buildable blueprint or verified engineering documentation. |
| Thermal Infrared | False-color zones are distinct and intentional; Horizon and major silhouettes remain recognizable; There are no invented temperature readings or measurement overlays | False colors are artistic choices; ordinary photographs do not establish temperature, emissivity or measured infrared radiation. |
| Wabi-Sabi | Material wear feels restrained and natural; Asymmetry and empty space create a quiet composition; Key source forms remain legible after simplification | A broad visual interpretation of weathering and restraint; it does not represent the full cultural or philosophical meaning of wabi-sabi. |
| Webcore | The source architecture and framing remain recognizable; Dither and mesh effects are localized; Decorative accents support the image without pretending to be functional UI | A static bitmap aesthetic; it is not a functional website, UI implementation, exact pixel grid or guaranteed readable text. |
| X-Ray Transparency | Overlapping transparent layers remain readable; The original silhouette and main spatial relationships survive; Interior lines read as an artistic interpretation | Invented interior layers are artistic; this is not a real X-ray, inspection result or accurate structural disclosure. |
| Xpiritualism | Particles and gauzy layers follow a coherent source form; The luminous focal mass has enough dark separation; The treatment stays abstract without adding a deity or human figure | A descriptive aesthetic label, not a standardized art movement or evidence of spiritual phenomena. |
