# Visual acceptance

Check every output against the brief and accepted anchor. Record concrete defects rather than a numerical “style match” claim without a calibrated measurement.

| Recipe | Look for | Do not promise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASCII/pixels | Glyph-like stippling or intentional blocks; restrained palette; readable silhouette | Valid ASCII text, exact glyphs, pixel-grid correctness, game-ready sprite tiles |
| Sketch/scribble | Gestural line hierarchy, visible paper, controlled accent marks | An exact artist's drawing or preserved strokes after edits |
| Digital impressionism | Broken-color marks, warm light/cool shadows, consistent brush scale | Pixel-identical brushwork or exact geometry |
| Lo-fi film | Restrained grain, soft focus, gentle vignette and subtle light leaks | Real camera/film simulation or exact colorimetry |
| Generative flow | Coherent contours, layered filaments and intentional empty space | Executable algorithms, seed reproducibility, seamless tiling |
| Off-white | Warm neutral values, subtle texture, sparse composition | Accessible UI text contrast or a usable website as a bitmap |

For transparent cutouts, verify edges and that transparent pixels exist. For requested text, inspect every word. If exact lettering is required, flag errors and propose a separate deterministic typography step.

An inspection record states asset ID, checks performed, defect, severity, and whether repair is needed. A missing viewing tool means the visual check is unverified, not passed.
