## Practical limits

- Atmospheric fantasy treatment; it cannot restore hidden detail and does not certify a photographic location or physical lighting conditions.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The treatment is close to atmospheric photography and does not strongly blur all foliage edges.
