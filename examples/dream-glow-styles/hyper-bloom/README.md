## Practical limits

- Flower forms and colors are imagined; this is not a botanical record or a guarantee of realistic plant morphology.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The plants and flowers are heavily embellished and the lighting is warmer than the recipe's mint accents.
