## Practical limits

- A nostalgic illustration aesthetic, not a calibrated film process or an exact reconstruction of any historical advertisement.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The source remains relatively photographic; the visible star glints and stronger sun glow are stylized additions.
