## Practical limits

- Wash edges and tonal detail may drift during edits; physical ink diffusion is not simulated.
- Palette and fine geometry are prompt guidance and require visual review across a series.
- The sky is densely washed rather than mostly empty paper.
- Fine cloud edges and roof details are interpreted.
