## Practical limits

- No fabric cutting template or stitch pattern is supplied.
- Palette and fine geometry are prompt guidance and require visual review across a series.
- Foliage is more detailed than the suggested large-shape simplification.
- Individual fabric shapes are illustrative, not validated cutting templates.
