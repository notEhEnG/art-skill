## Practical limits

- Raster illustration only; no vector paths or exact polygon topology is supplied.
- Palette and fine geometry are prompt guidance and require visual review across a series.
- Small details such as the distant boat are simplified or indistinct.
- Color planes are raster marks, not supplied vector polygons.
