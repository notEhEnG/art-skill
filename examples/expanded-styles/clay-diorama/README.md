## Practical limits

- Raster visualization only; no 3D model, mesh, construction plan or physically accurate material is supplied.
- Palette and fine geometry are prompt guidance and require visual review across a series.
- The cloud retains softer wispy edges than the strongly modeled building forms.
- Small window and railing geometry is interpreted rather than exact.
