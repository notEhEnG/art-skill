## Practical limits

- A lighting aesthetic; it does not specify a calibrated light rig, product color accuracy or a physical miniature.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The lighting is a synthetic architectural reinterpretation rather than a documented studio setup.
