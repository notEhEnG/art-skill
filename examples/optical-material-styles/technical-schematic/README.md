## Practical limits

- An illustrative raster plate, not CAD, a dimensioned drawing, a buildable blueprint or verified engineering documentation.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The rainbow is reduced to construction arcs; guides and perspective are illustrative rather than measured.
