## Practical limits

- Fine shadow details may be deliberately lost; this is an artistic tonal treatment rather than recovered photographic information.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- Shadow detail is deliberately reduced and the upper wisps differ from the original.
