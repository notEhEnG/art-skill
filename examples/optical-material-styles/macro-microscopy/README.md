## Practical limits

- Cell-like structures are imagined; the image cannot identify species, demonstrate biological anatomy or substitute for microscopy.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The selected leaf and microscopic-looking compartments are synthesized, not resolved or measured from the source photograph.
- The result leans toward transmitted-light macro photography rather than a stained laboratory specimen.
