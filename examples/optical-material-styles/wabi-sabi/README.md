## Practical limits

- A broad visual interpretation of weathering and restraint; it does not represent the full cultural or philosophical meaning of wabi-sabi.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- The result retains substantial foliage detail and is less sparse than a minimal still-life interpretation.
