## Practical limits

- Refraction and caustics are visual approximations; no physically validated optical simulation or seamless water texture is supplied.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- Some cloud edges retain a gently rippled appearance, but the repeating sky-wide caustic mesh was removed.
- The repaired scene contains a more explicit sun glow than the source.
