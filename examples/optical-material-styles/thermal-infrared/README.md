## Practical limits

- False colors are artistic choices; ordinary photographs do not establish temperature, emissivity or measured infrared radiation.
- Palette, fine geometry and cross-image consistency require visual review; generation is not deterministic.
- False colors follow artistic value choices and convey no measured temperatures.
